Privacy Policy

Little Learners · Last updated: 23 November 2026

Little Learners is a learning app for children from age 2 to Grade 5. It is made to be used by children with a parent or guardian, and we collect as little information as possible.

Who we are

Little Learners is run by Nikhil Lattupalli. Questions about privacy: nikhillattupally@gmail.com.

Using the app without an account

You can use Little Learners without signing in. Everything — learner profiles, stars and worksheet results — is then saved only on your device and never sent to us.

What we store if a grown-up creates an account

Only a parent or guardian can create an account. Children never create accounts or enter email addresses. With an account we store:

InformationWhy
The grown-up's email address and a securely hashed password To sign in and to reset a forgotten password
For each child: a first name or nickname, a cartoon avatar, a colour and a grade level To show the right worksheets and keep each child's progress separate
Worksheet results: which worksheet, the date and time, stars, number of questions, mistakes and minutes spent For the grown-ups' progress report and to adjust difficulty
Short codes for questions a child has seen in the last 30 days So the same questions don't repeat too soon

We do not collect children's full names, photos, voice recordings, location, contacts, or device identifiers. There are no ads, no in-app purchases, no social features, and no third-party analytics or tracking.

Read-aloud

Questions are read aloud using the voice built into your device or browser. No recordings are made.

Where the data is kept

Account data is stored with our database provider, Supabase, on encrypted servers. Each family's data is protected so that only that family's signed-in grown-up can read or change it. We do not sell or share personal information with anyone.

Your choices

  • See your data: the 📊 Progress report in the Grown-ups area shows everything we store about each child.
  • Edit or delete a child: Grown-ups → tap the child.
  • Delete everything: Grown-ups → ☁️ Account → Delete account and all data. This permanently removes the account, all children and all results from our servers immediately.
  • You can also email us to have your data deleted.

Children's privacy (COPPA)

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Accounts are created by parents or guardians, and the information about children listed above is provided by the parent. Grown-up areas of the app are behind a parent check. If you believe a child has given us personal information without a parent's consent, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above and, for important changes, tell signed-in families in the app.
